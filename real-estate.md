# Buying or Selling on Long Island

Thinking about buying or selling a home? Let's talk about what's actually
possible, no pressure.

I'm Sammi Gropper, a Licensed Real Estate Salesperson with **The Lenard
Team at Signature Premier Properties**, based out of our Smithtown office.
I blend the same care I bring to lactation support and my kids' books into
helping you find the right place to call home: layout over square
footage, storage over finishes, buy for your life and your season, not
the algorithm.

I serve Huntington, Northport, Centerport, Greenlawn, East Northport,
Commack, Smithtown, and all of Long Island.
