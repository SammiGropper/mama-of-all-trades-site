About Me

Hi, I'm Sammi 🤍

I'm a mom of two (hi Remi and Cole!), married to my high school sweetheart Zach, and a Long Island girl through and through. Born and raised in Merrick, now raising my family in Huntington.

I spent years teaching K-5 STEM. Then I became a mom, started pumping, and the only advice my OB gave me was to follow someone on Instagram. So I got certified instead. Now I'm a Certified Breastfeeding Counselor and pumping specialist, and I've helped 100+ moms find a plan that actually works for their life.

I'm also a real estate agent with Signature Premier Properties, a 3x children's book author, and co-host of the Struggle Out Loud podcast. Mama of all trades, for real.

You're not doing it wrong. And you don't have to do it alone.

xo, Sammi
